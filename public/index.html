Simple by design

One place for the school day

Connect once, then move between the information and tools you use most.

  1. 01

    Connect PowerSchool

    Sign in from the desktop or Android app. Your schedule and saved account data stay on your device.

  2. 02

    See what is next

    Check the current class, start and end times, teacher, room, countdown, and the next lesson at a glance.

  3. 03

    Keep tasks clear

    Add a title and detailed information, reopen a task when you need the context, and edit it from the app or desktop Tasks card.

  4. 04

    Use the built-in tools

    Translate text or screen captures, keep useful schedule cards on the Windows desktop, and use the app's connection tools when needed.
